## Repository interpretation

This wrapper does not reproduce the canonical design/verification system. It is
an original distribution seam whose job is to detect PPT/PPTX beautification
intent, obtain or fast-forward the canonical public repository, run its official
installer, and then defer to the installed canonical `SKILL.md` as the single
source of truth.

## Quality boundary

The wrapper preserves the canonical project's fail-closed policy. It does not
claim that installation succeeded when Git/network/filesystem access is blocked,
and it does not relax Content Lock, source identity, font portability, Global
Design Jury, or full-deck no-regression requirements.

## Excluded

- Paid, members-only, credentialed, or private source material
- Proprietary presentation templates or third-party brand assets
- Silent reconstruction of inaccessible content
- Any bootstrap behavior that bypasses host authorization or filesystem policy

## External design research boundary

The canonical project may cite public design principles and presentation
benchmarks. This catalog wrapper itself does not redistribute third-party
presentation templates, proprietary assets, paid prompts, or members-only
material.
