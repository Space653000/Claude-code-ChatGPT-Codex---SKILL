## Repository interpretation

This skill turns the user's standing policy into a reusable cross-platform
distillation workflow. Its source gate, duplicate check, provenance contract,
automatic invocation requirement, and host-neutral design are original
repository instructions.

## Excluded

- Private or credentialed sources
- Attempts to reconstruct unavailable material
- Wholesale copying of publicly readable expression
